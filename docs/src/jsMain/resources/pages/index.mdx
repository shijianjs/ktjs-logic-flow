<ktjs-features section-title="Why write animations in Kotlin" tinted>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:workflow" title="Graph in, graph out">`render` takes nodes and edges as typed builders, `getGraphRawData` hands the same shape back.</ktjs-feature>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:mouse-pointer-2" title="Editing surface">`addNode`, `addEdge`, `deleteElement`, the selection calls, and custom properties on either element kind.</ktjs-feature>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:radio" title="Typed event names">`LogicFlowEvents` covers 30 names — `node:click`, `node:add`, `edge:add`, `graph:rendered`, `history:change`, …</ktjs-feature>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:move" title="Viewport and history">`zoom`, `fitView`, `translate`, `getTransform`, plus `undo` / `redo` over the built-in history.</ktjs-feature>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:flask-conical" title="Tested in Chromium">Eight browser tests drive a real editor, and seven Playwright checks drive the demo page.</ktjs-feature>

<ktjs-feature icon="lucide:book-open" title="Docs in two languages">Every page exists in English and Simplified Chinese, and links out to the demo and the Dokka reference.</ktjs-feature>

</ktjs-features>
